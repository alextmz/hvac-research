# Stage 4 — Reliability, Serviceability and Australian Pricing

## Goal

Resolve ownership-risk and value questions for the serious contender set without wasting effort on systems already eliminated for engineering reasons.

## Reliability/service research

Assess, with evidence scope made explicit:

- exact-model/generation recurring faults where available;
- shared-platform evidence where exact-model data is sparse;
- inverter/PCB/compressor/fan/control failures;
- generation age and maturity;
- parts availability and typical lead-time signals in Australia;
- manufacturer/distributor service network and technical support;
- warranty term, inclusions and recurring warranty-service problems;
- installer/technician and long-term owner experiences.

Do not turn generic brand reputation into exact-model fact. Distinguish exact-model, shared-platform and general-brand evidence.

Community sources such as Reddit, Whirlpool/OzBargain/OCAU and trade/owner forums are useful for repeated failure patterns and support experiences, but treat anecdotes as such and look for independent repetition. Do not use aggregate star ratings as reliability evidence.

## Pricing

For meaningful contenders, collect preferably >=3 current Australian observations where economically available.

Record separately:

- equipment-only price;
- installed price/quote where configuration is sufficiently clear;
- seller/location;
- stock/availability;
- observation date;
- inclusions/exclusions that materially affect comparability.

Lowest observed price is not automatically the normal market price. Derive a practical “good price” range from comparable current observations and flag unusually low or non-comparable listings.

For local purchasing decisions, proximity is secondary to price/value; do not discard a materially better price merely because the seller is farther away.

## Research strategy

Work only the surviving/serious contender set plus any close contender whose reliability or price could materially change its position. Reuse platform-level evidence carefully rather than repeating identical research for every exact pairing.

## Stopping rule

This stage is ready when each serious contender has enough reliability/service evidence to identify material ownership risks and enough current pricing evidence for a defensible value comparison, or explicit evidence gaps/blockers are recorded.
